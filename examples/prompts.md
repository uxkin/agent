# Example prompts

Things to ask your agent once UXKIN is connected. Each one names UXKIN,
so the agent knows to search before it builds.

## A new screen

> Use UXKIN to find how three real apps design their subscription
> paywall. Tell me what they have in common and where they differ, then
> build ours using the parts that fit our app.

> Before you build the settings screen, look up real settings screens on
> UXKIN from apps similar to ours. Keep our existing components.

## A whole flow

> Find an onboarding journey on UXKIN from a habit or fitness app and
> walk me through it screen by screen. Then list which steps our
> onboarding is missing.

> Use UXKIN to show me how real apps let people cancel a subscription.
> Compare them with our cancel flow and suggest changes.

## Empty, error and edge states

> Look up real empty states on UXKIN for lists and search results, then
> design ours for the projects page.

> How do real apps show a failed payment? Find examples on UXKIN and
> write the error state for our checkout.

## A website or landing page

> Find a light, minimal SaaS design system on UXKIN and use its colors,
> type scale and button style for our landing page hero.

> Use UXKIN to pull the DESIGN.md of a design system that suits a
> developer tool, then restyle our pricing page to match it.

## Reviewing what's already built

> Compare our signup screen with real signup screens on UXKIN and give
> me the three changes that would help most.

> Check this page against the no-ui-slop rules and tell me what looks
> AI-generated, then fix it.

## Your saved collections

> List my UXKIN collections and use the references in "Checkout ideas"
> to redesign the cart page.
