# website-design

**Website Design** by [UXKIN](https://uxkin.com). Real UI, not AI slop.

```sh
npx website-design
```

Design and build websites and landing pages that look like a real product, not AI output. Adds the landing-page, web-design-system and web-review skills to Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and other AI coding tools, and connects 15k+ real website design systems from UXKIN.

## What you get

- **web-design-system**: choose a design direction from real websites and write it down as a DESIGN.md
- **landing-page**: build landing, pricing, feature and waitlist pages that look like a real product
- **web-review**: review a website and list what to fix, most important first

The skills go into the AI coding tools on your computer that you choose (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, GitHub Copilot, Windsurf, Antigravity, Kiro and more). Then UXKIN is connected to them, so the skills can look at real products first. Sign in to UXKIN once in each tool; searching needs a [UXKIN plan](https://uxkin.com/choose-plan). The skills also work on their own.

Then ask your AI, for example: *"Build a landing page for my app"*.

## Options

```sh
npx website-design --skills-only   # just the skills, no UXKIN connection
npx website-design --yes           # don't ask: all your AI tools, for your user
```

The same as `npx uxkin add website-design`. More: [`uxkin`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/uxkin) (`npx uxkin --help`).

Requires Node.js 18.17 or newer.
