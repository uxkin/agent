Design or improve the paywall for this iPhone app. Extra context from the person: $ARGUMENTS

1. Use the `ios-flows` skill to decide where the paywall appears, look at how comparable real apps present plans and trials (with the UXKIN tools if they're connected) and plan the screen.
2. The paywall must show the price and billing period clearly, what's included, Restore Purchases, the terms and privacy links, and a visible way to close it where the App Store guidelines require one. Never invent prices or plans; ask for them.
3. Show the person the plan and wait for their go-ahead, then build it with the `ios-screens` skill and check it on a small and a large iPhone, in light and dark mode and at the largest text size.
